1.1.1 FromBrowsertoWebPage—TheFullJourney
WhenavisitortypesaURLintotheirbrowserandpressesEnter,acomplexsequenceofevents
happensinunderonesecond.Understandingthissequenceisthefoundationofallhostingknowledge.
1. Thevisitor'sbrowserextractsthe domain namefrom the URL—e.g.'example.co.ke'
2. Thebrowserasks aDNS Resolver(usually provided by the ISP)totranslate that domain nameinto
anIPaddress
3. TheDNSResolverqueries authoritative nameservers andreturns an IPaddress —e.g.
102.215.8.45
4. Thebrowseropens aTCPconnection toport80 (HTTP)orport443(HTTPS)on thatIPaddress
5. Theweb serversoftware(Apacheor Nginx)atthatIPreceivesthe requestand locatesthe
requestedfile
6. Theserverprocessesany server-side code (PHP,Python,Node.js)andconstructs aresponse
7. Theserversends the HTMLresponse backtothe browser,whichrenders itas aweb page
WhyThisMattersforSupport
Everycommonhostingcomplaintmapstooneofthesesevensteps.'Sitenot loading'=usually Step3(DNS)
orStep5(webserver).'Blank whitepage'=Step6(PHPerror).'SSLwarning' =Step4(certificateissue).
Knowingthe journeytells youexactly wheretolook.

1.1.4 DataCentresandServerInfrastructure
Professionalhostingserversliveinpurpose-builtfacilitiescalleddatacentres.Understandingdata
centreconceptshelpswhenadvisingclientsonserverlocation,latency,andcompliancerequirements.
• TierClassification —Datacentres arerated TierI(basic)toTierIV(fault-tolerantwith 99.995%
uptimeSLA).Enterprisehosting runsonTierIII orIVfacilities.
• PowerRedundancy —TierIII+facilitieshaveN+1 UPSsystemsand dieselgenerators tomaintain
powerduring outages.
• NetworkRedundancy —Multiple upstream ISPconnections (multi-homing)ensure the facilitystays
onlineif oneproviderfails.
• Cooling—Servers generate enormous heat;data centresuse precisionairconditioningand hot/cold
aislecontainment.
• PhysicalSecurity—Biometric access control,CCTV,security personnel, and cage systemsprotect
hardwarefromphysical tampering.
• LatencyConsiderations —Aserverin Nairobiserves Kenyan visitors fasterthan one in London.For
abusinessprimarily serving EastAfrican customers,chooseservers inornearthe region(e.g.,
NairobiIXP-connected datacentres).

1.3.1 HowDNSResolutionWorks—StepbyStep
WhenabrowserneedstofindtheIPaddressfor'example.co.ke',itgoesthroughastructuredquery
process:
17.Browserchecksitslocal cache—has italreadylookedthis uprecently?
18.Ifnotcached, itqueries the OperatingSystem'sDNSresolver (configuredin /etc/resolv.conf on
Linux)
19.TheOSresolverchecksits cache,thenqueriesaRecursive Resolver(e.g.,8.8.8.8 forGoogle DNS)
20.TheRecursiveResolverqueriesaRootNameservertofind the.ke TLDnameservers
21.Itqueriesthe .keTLDserver tofind the authoritativenameservers for'example.co.ke'
22.Itqueriesthoseauthoritativenameservers and retrievesthe Arecord with theIPaddress
23.TheIPisreturned tothe browserand cached(forthe TTLduration)toavoid repeatingthis process
